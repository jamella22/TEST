## 🏢 BANFICO - Site Reliability & L3 Support Engineer

### Company Overview
- **Industry**: FinTech / SaaS
- **Product**: Verification of Payee & Open Finance solutions
- **Status**: Bootstrapped, profitable
- **Clients**: 200+ financial institutions (banks)
- **Presence**: Europe, LATAM, Middle East

### Position Summary
Primarily an advanced L2/L3 support role, acting as bridge between frontline support and DevOps/SRE teams. Handle complex incidents, support live SaaS platforms in production, gain exposure to site reliability practices.

---

### 🎯 Key Responsibilities

#### Support & Incident Management
- Own escalated (L2/L3) support tickets from clients
- Ensure timely analysis and resolution
- Manage L1 and L2 tickets in Jira Service Management
- Participate in rotating on-call schedule (additional compensation)

#### Troubleshooting & Analysis
- Troubleshoot at infrastructure, application, and integration levels
- Identify root causes and work with engineering to implement fixes
- Document incident reports, service requests, and follow-up actions

#### Collaboration
- Work cross-functionally with Customer Success, Product, Development, and DevOps teams
- Handle customer-reported issues, escalations, and production incidents

#### Monitoring & Reliability
- Proactively monitor systems using Prometheus, Grafana, EFK stack
- Anticipate issues before they escalate
- Operate and maintain infrastructure

#### Automation & Infrastructure
- Work with Kubernetes/OpenShift, Ansible, Terraform, Elastic
- Optimize build and deploy processes

---

### ✅ Required Skills & Experience

#### Experience
- **2-5 years** in advanced technical support, production operations, or system administration
- SaaS or FinTech environment preferred
- Proven ability to analyze, troubleshoot, and resolve distributed, containerized systems

#### Technical Skills
- **Linux** (OS and CLI) - strong experience
- **Ansible** (automation/configuration management)
- **Monitoring**: Grafana, Prometheus, ELK/EFK or similar
- **Cloud**: AWS (hands-on experience)
- **Containers**: Understanding of containerization

#### Soft Skills
- Collaborative mindset - work with technical and non-technical teams
- Excellent communication - explain technical issues clearly
- Willingness to learn, adapt, and take ownership
- Works well in dynamic, fast-paced environment

#### Bonus
- Previous experience in regulated environment (financial services)
- Willingness to work flexible schedule including potential 24x7 support shifts

---

### 💰 Benefits

- Opportunity to progress as organization grows
- Work in recession-proof, disruptive business segment
- Remote flexibility within European Union

---

### 📍 Other Details

#### Work Authorization
- Banfico doesn't have legal entity outside Germany
- Can engage as **contractor (B2B)** or via **EoR (Employer of Record)**
- No visa sponsorship
- Standard working hours: European core hours

#### On-Call
- Currently no shift-based system (may change in future)
- On-call support is rare but may be required
- Shared across team and compensated additionally:
  - Paid for on-call availability
  - Paid extra for actual interventions

#### Behavioral Topics

- Implement integration of extensible services and platforms

- Ensure compliance with high security standards including:
  - Inventory management
