### IT Operations Monitoring & Compliance — Mission and Qualifications

Date: 2025-10-14

---

### Mission
- Maintain and enhance the monitoring system (Grafana, VictoriaMetrics, Loki).
- Monitor and validate technical compliance of IT services, systems, and infrastructure.
- Manage alert and incident processes to ensure timely resolution.
- Support and maintain the Configuration Management Database (CMDB).
- Collect and aggregate operational data to support decision-making.
- Automate processes to improve efficiency and reduce manual effort.
- Coordinate remediation of compliance gaps across IT services.
- Maintain accurate operational documentation and contribute to the internal knowledge base.

### Qualifications
- Have at least 2 years of experience in an IT corporate environment as an IT Administrator, DevOps Engineer, or Site Reliability Engineer.
- Have hands-on experience with monitoring and log collection tools (Grafana, VictoriaMetrics, Loki).
- Have solid Linux and Docker administration experience.
- Communicate effectively in English, both verbally and in writing.
- Understand corporate IT environments, technologies, and solutions.
- Have experience working with CMDB systems.
- Are curious, proactive, and eager to learn new tools and technologies.

### Bonus
- Practical experience with Kubernetes.
- Familiarity with non-mainstream tools and a strong learning attitude.
- A collaborative mindset and ability to work across teams.

### Culture
- Innovation, curiosity, and continuous learning.
- Diversity, inclusion, and transparency.
- Teamwork and knowledge sharing.
